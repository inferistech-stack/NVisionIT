// Our story

Started in St. Louis. Trusted nationwide.

Nvision IT was founded in 2004 with a vision of transforming today's business systems challenges into tomorrow's successes. Although information technology is what we're known for, we consider the underlying business processes in everything we do — focusing on our clients' strategic goals and building them into the solutions we recommend.

We grew quickly — earning a spot among America's fastest-growing companies — by consistently delivering projects that actually worked. Today we're an Oracle-certified Gold Partner and an internationally quality-certified firm headquartered in Chesterfield, Missouri, serving mid-sized to Fortune 500 companies across the public, private and governmental sectors.

Industries served include defense, healthcare, education, manufacturing, technology, telecom, utilities and transportation.

Through all of it, our focus has never changed: solve the real problem, deliver on what we promised, and stand behind the result.
