Each certification below is an official stamp of approval that makes hiring us simpler. Here's what every one actually means — in plain English.

Pre-Approved

Approved Government Contractor (GSA Schedule)

We're on the GSA Schedule — a pre-negotiated list of vendors the government has already vetted. Agencies can hire us directly, skipping a long, competitive bidding process.

Official reference: GSA MAS Contract GS-35F-414DA
IT Contract

Authorized Federal IT Provider (STARS III)

We hold a spot on STARS III, a government-wide contract built specifically for technology services. It gives agencies a fast, pre-approved way to bring us in for IT work.

Official reference: GSA 8(a) STARS III · 47QTCB22D0369
Small Business

Federally Certified Small Business (SBA 8(a))

We're certified under the SBA's 8(a) program for small, disadvantaged businesses. Agencies that work with us help meet their small-business goals — and can award certain contracts to us directly.

Status: SBA 8(a) Certified · Minority Business Enterprise (MBE) · DOT DBE
Verified Vendor

Registered Federal Contractor (CAGE Code)

A CAGE Code is the unique ID the U.S. government assigns to approved vendors. Ours confirms we're officially registered and ready to be awarded federal contracts.

Our code: CAGE 6LV78
