// Contract vehicles

Two fast paths to bring us on board.

GSA

Multiple Award Schedule (MAS)

The General Services Administration's IT MAS is the government's go-to source for IT acquisitions, recognized for cost savings, time savings, selection and built-in value. It sits under GSA's Federal Acquisition Service (FAS) and Office of Integrated Technology Services (ITS).

IT Schedule is an Indefinite Delivery, Indefinite Quantity (IDIQ) contract offering a comprehensive array of state-of-the-art IT products, services and solutions. Approximately 80 percent of IT MAS contract holders are small businesses.

Contract: GS-35F-414DA · More on GSA IT MAS ↗
GWAC

8(a) STARS III

GSA's 8(a) Streamlined Technology Acquisition Resources for Services (STARS) III is a Multiple Award, Indefinite-Delivery, Indefinite-Quantity (MA-IDIQ) Governmentwide Acquisition Contract for IT services and emerging technology-based solutions, plus the ancillary support integral to them.

It is a five-year contract with one three-year option — a $50 billion vehicle set aside exclusively for SBA-certified 8(a) prime contractors. Built on GSA's long-standing partnership with the SBA, it gives agencies a way to deliver mission-critical IT services with small, disadvantaged businesses and supports Executive Order 13985 on advancing equity and support for underserved communities.

Contract: 47QTCB22D0369 · gsa.gov/s3 ↗
